Jiuhai Chen

I received my Ph.D. in Computer Science from the University of Maryland in January 2026, where I was advised by Tianyi Zhou and Tom Goldstein.

My research centers on multimodal and large language models — including visual perception, visual generation, and reasoning across both MLLMs and LLMs.
